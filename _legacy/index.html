About Dialight FA

Dialight Football Academy is a youth football academy based in Adura, Alagbado, Lagos. It was founded and is owned by Coach Imole Tobi, and it trains players in four age groups: Under 10, Under 13, Under 17 and free age.

Our motto is “Breeding the Future Stars”. We put players first, build them up on the pitch, and help them find the next step, whether that is a local league, a national team or a club abroad.
